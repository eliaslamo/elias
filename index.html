Everything you want to know before getting started.

A senior full-time designer costs $100k–$160k per year in salary alone — before benefits, equipment, and management overhead. With elias.co, you get senior-level design quality at a fraction of the cost, with the flexibility to pause or cancel anytime. No hiring risk, no onboarding lag, no vacation days.

Most requests are delivered within 1–2 business days. More complex work like full website designs or comprehensive brand identities may take 3–5 days. We set expectations clearly upfront so you're never left wondering.

We offer unlimited revisions — we iterate until you're genuinely thrilled with the result. Our goal is work that you're proud to show off, so we won't stop until we get there. In practice, most clients are happy after one or two rounds of feedback.

All design work is produced in Figma, which means you'll receive fully editable source files you own forever. We can also export to any format you need — PDF, PNG, SVG, and more. No proprietary formats, no lock-in.
